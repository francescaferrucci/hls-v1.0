HANNAH LEARNING SYSTEM — LEVELS 1–12 FULL PROTOTYPE

FOCUS OF THIS BUILD
Level 10 — Member Communication
Level 11 — Advanced Medicine
Level 12 — Hannah Standards of Excellence

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip the archive.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
3. Select Academies > Hannah Medical Academy.
4. Open Levels 10, 11, or 12.

LEVEL 10 INCLUDES
- Six complete course prototypes
- Communication Practice Lab
- Four simulations
- Competency Passport
- Six job aids

LEVEL 11 INCLUDES
- Oncology, Neurology, Ophthalmology, Cardiology, Ultrasound, Advanced Anesthesia, ICU, and conditional Mechanical Ventilation
- Advanced Case Conference and ICU Prioritization Lab
- Four simulations
- Competency Passport and eight job aids

LEVEL 12 INCLUDES
- Diagnostic Workflow Hub
- Hannahware Clinical Documentation
- Hospitalization Board
- Treatment Timeline
- Outside Diagnostic Approval
- Medical Records Workflow
- Cycle of Service in Medical
- Discharge Excellence
- Phone Triage Standards
- Quality Assurance Audits
- Connected Workflow Map and interactive Quality Audit
- Four simulations, Competency Passport, and ten job aids

COURSE FRAMEWORK
Every course includes why it matters, learning objectives, foundational knowledge, the Hannah standard workflow, an interactive decision, knowledge-check evidence, simulation application, competency validation, and a job aid.

DATA & PROGRESS
Prototype progress is stored locally in the browser using localStorage. No server is required.

CLINICAL GOVERNANCE
This is an educational product prototype. Final production clinical content, thresholds, medications, doses, interventions, equipment procedures, role permissions, and specialty standards must be reviewed and approved by Hannah clinical leadership. Mechanical Ventilation is explicitly conditional on Hannah offering that service with approved equipment, protocols, and training.
